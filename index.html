Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12-05-2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StockDesk collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our stock tracking mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Basic Information: Name, email, or company details (if provided).
  • Stock Data: Barcode scan data, stock in/out entries, item details, timestamps, and related inventory records.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, app version, and diagnostic logs.
  • Camera Access: Used only for scanning barcodes.
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns to improve performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To scan barcodes and update stock (IN/OUT).
  • To maintain accurate inventory records.
  • To improve app performance and reliability.
  • To provide support and resolve issues.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data, including secure storage and transmission. However, no system is 100% secure.

Data may be stored locally on the device and synchronized with servers when internet is available.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your data. We may share data with:

  • Your organization (for inventory management).
  • Service providers (hosting, analytics).
  • Authorities if required by law.

5. Permissions Used

  • Camera: Required for barcode scanning.
  • Storage (if applicable): To save scan data or reports.

6. Your Rights

  • You can request access or deletion of your data.
  • You can disable permissions from device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13.
